Create a Lobby

Enter up to 10 player names. Optionally pick a role for anyone who wants a specific one, a preferred hero, and use "Keep with / apart" to group friends together or split up rivals - leave any of these on its default to let the shuffle and rolls decide. Teams are shuffled automatically into Team A / Team B, with a locked 1 Tank / 2 Damage / 2 Support comp per team. A preferred hero is used whenever that player rolls as long as it matches their role and isn't already taken on their team - otherwise it's rolled at random like normal. Swap roles between players any time, and reshuffle teams for a new game without retyping names. No two players on the same team will get the same hero. Optionally cap how many times each player can reroll their own build - leave it blank for unlimited rerolls.
